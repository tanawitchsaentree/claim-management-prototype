# Rewrites: before and after

Load this file when the output matters (application answers, cover notes, essays, anything the user will send to another human) or when your draft keeps tripping the banlist. These pairs are the calibration target. Match the AFTER voice.

---

## English

### Cover note / application answer

BEFORE (AI voice):
"I'm excited to apply for this role — it's not just a job for me, it's an opportunity to leverage my passion for design. Throughout my journey, I've delivered seamless, robust, and user-centric solutions that empower teams to move faster. I believe my unique blend of creativity, technical skill, and strategic thinking makes me an ideal fit."

AFTER (human voice):
"I've spent the last two years designing the kind of tool this role describes: a hospital records system that nurses use through entire shifts. I was the only designer, so I built the design system myself. It now runs in ten hospitals. I also write production front-end code, which means my designs usually arrive as working builds."

What changed: every claim became a checkable fact. The dash went. The triad went. The self-ratings ("unique blend", "ideal fit") went, because the reader decides those.

### Explaining a decision

BEFORE:
"We chose this approach not because it was easy, but because it was right. It's a game-changer that will transform how the team works — unlocking new levels of productivity."

AFTER:
"We chose this approach because the old one made engineers wait three weeks for every rule change. This removes the wait. That's the whole argument."

### Email declining something

BEFORE:
"Thank you so much for reaching out! While I truly appreciate the opportunity, I regret to inform you that I won't be able to participate at this time. I hope this helps, and please don't hesitate to reach out in the future!"

AFTER:
"Thanks for thinking of me. I can't take this on right now, my plate is full through March. If the timing were different I'd say yes, so please ask again next time."

## Thai

BEFORE (โทน AI):
"โปรเจกต์นี้ไม่ใช่แค่การออกแบบหน้าจอ แต่คือการยกระดับประสบการณ์ผู้ใช้อย่างไร้รอยต่อ — ตอบโจทย์ทั้งความเร็ว ความชัดเจน และความน่าเชื่อถือ ในยุคที่ AI เข้ามามีบทบาทในทุกมิติ"

## The read-aloud test, applied

Take any sentence from your draft and imagine saying it across a desk to a colleague. These fail:

- "This solution seamlessly integrates with your existing workflow." (Nobody talks like this.)
- "It's not about the tools — it's about the mindset." (Contrast scaffold plus dash.)
- "ระบบนี้ถือเป็นก้าวสำคัญในการยกระดับองค์กร" (สคริปต์พิธีกร ไม่ใช่คนคุยงาน)

These pass:

- "It plugs into the workflow you already have. Nothing to migrate."
- "The tools matter less than you'd think. Most of the win came from changing when we review, from mockups to working builds."
- "ระบบนี้ทำให้ทีมเลิกรอกันได้จริง อันนี้คือกำไรหลัก"

## Direct-punchy register (load references/voice.md first)

BEFORE (generated "engaging" voice):
"In today's fast-paced design landscape, it's worth noting that AI-powered prototyping isn't just a trend — it's a fundamental shift. The result? Designers who embrace these tools can seamlessly iterate faster, unlock new levels of productivity, and elevate their impact."

AFTER (a person with an opinion, typing):
"You're testing ideas too slowly. A working build now costs an afternoon. Put it in front of five users this week, watch where they stall, fix it, show them again. The designers getting hired right now are the ones who compress weeks into days. You can argue with that, or you can get faster."

What changed: the dash died, "isn't just a trend" failed the strawman test and died, the triad died, all six lexicon words died. What replaced them is a claim, numbers, and a concession-pivot ending. Notice the rhythm: 6, 8, 18, 15, 12 words. Nothing uniform.
