## Who

Claim handler (commercial lines), registering a new loss (FNOL).
- Knows: the client, the policy they searched, what happened (cause, damage type, date, place).
- Often does not know: which other policies of the same client also cover this loss. That is exactly what the coverage check tells them.
- Juggling: a caller or broker email, several FNOLs a day, getting reserves right, not opening wrong or duplicate claims.
- Frequency: FNOL is daily. Multi-policy losses are rarer (assumption: a few per week per handler, more in mass events).
- Stakes: every ticked policy is a real claim with its own reserves and reporting (IMP-2). A wrong tick is work to undo; a missed tick is a gap in cover.

## Story

As a claim handler, I want to see which of the client's policies are affected by this loss and choose which ones get a claim, so that every covered policy is handled from one notification and I do not open claims nobody needs.

Real-world trigger
- A fire at a client site damages the building (Property) and injures a visitor (Liability). The handler starts from the Property policy.
- After the cause and damage type are entered, the coverage check (Conclusion Engine, Confluence 3341441184 section 2.2) finds the Liability and Engineering policies too.

Today in production the hint is a closable banner on Entities & Damages, "Other policies may be impacted", with a See details dialog to add them (impacted-policies.component.html, add-policies-modal.component.html). It is easy to close and miss. The prototype turns it into a step.

## Journey

1. Enter Loss information
- Sees: the FNOL form and a stepper with Loss information, Entities & Damages, Parties, Reserves, Summary.
- Knows: the loss facts. Believes: next comes Entities & Damages.
- Does: fills cause, damage type, dates, presses Next.
- Feels: routine.

2. Coverage check runs
- Sees: "Checking the client's other policies" spinner (AC-01, AC-06).
- Believes: something is being looked up for them.
- Feels: brief wait, should be short and named.

3. Policies step opens
- Sees: a new step in the stepper and one table: the searched policy ticked and badged, suggested policies unticked, each with a "Coverage match" reason (AC-01, IMP-2).
- Knows: the client and the loss. Does not yet know: whether these extra policies really apply.
- Does: reads each reason, ticks the ones that apply.
- Feels: helped if reasons are clear; wary, because ticking means extra claims.

4. Optional: search for a policy that was not suggested
- Sees: "Search for another policy" dialog, client name filled in (AC-04, IMP-4).
- Does: searches, ticks, "Add to list". Sees a toast and the row badged "Added by you".
- Feels: in control, as long as the first search shows something useful.

5. Confirm the choice
- Sees: "Create a claim on each of N policies?" naming every policy (AC-02, IMP-3). Only when the result differs from a normal FNOL.
- Does: Continue, or Back to selection.
- Feels: safe if the dialog says exactly what will happen, including what is left out.

6. Entities & Damages
- Sees: a toast, and entities of every selected policy, each extra one tagged with its policy (AC-02).
- Does: picks entities and damages per policy.
- Feels: should be clear which entity belongs to which claim.

7. Back and change, until Submit
- Does: Back to Policies, ticks or unticks (IMP-1).
- Needs: to be told if unticking throws away work they did on Entities & Damages.

8. Summary and Submit
- Sees: "Claims to be created", one entry per policy; each with the total reserve entered on its own Reserves card (AC-05; IMP-7 changed 2026-11-23, see fnol-reserves-multi-claim.md).
- Does: Submit claim.

9. Result
- Sees: "N claims created" with claim links, or "2 of 3 claims created" with a failed row and Retry (AC-05, AC-05b, IMP-8).
- Does: retries, opens each claim.
- Feels: done only when every policy has a claim number in view.

## Pain points

- The hint is easy to miss today: production shows a closable info banner on Entities & Damages (impacted-policies.component.html). Closing it hides the policies for good.
- Every tick is a claim with reserves (IMP-2). Over-ticking creates work and wrong reporting.
- Suggestions can be wrong for real reasons: date of loss not in the other policy's period, location group not matching (Confluence 2397409328, policy flow rows 1 to 4). The reason text must make that visible.
- The handler cannot see the loss facts on the Policies step, so they judge the "Coverage match" text from memory (assumption).
- Unticking the searched policy means the policy the notification started from gets no claim. That is unusual and must be said out loud.
- Other client, other currency (IMP-5): allowed, but a wrong-client claim is a serious error.
- Going back and unticking after picking entities throws away those choices (policy-selection.service.ts applySelection removes the entities).
- In production, adding policies resets and re-concludes the AiP (impacted-policies.service.ts incidentRecord/reset, Confluence 3341441184 section 2.2). Work done before that can change (assumption that the prototype does not model this).
- Partial failure at Submit (IMP-8): some claims exist, some not. The handler must not lose track of which.
- Duplicate claims: a second Submit after the first went through.

## Needs

- Step 2: a named wait, short.
- Step 3: why each policy is suggested, in plain words; what the loss was (cause, damage, date) to judge it; a live count of claims that will be created.
- Step 4: a first search that shows something useful; a clear "already in the list"; a way to undo an added policy.
- Step 5: a confirm that names every policy that gets a claim, and any policy that does not (especially the searched one), and any mixed client or currency.
- Step 6: a clear link between each entity and its policy (which claim it lands on).
- Step 7: a warning before chosen entities are removed.
- Step 8: a confirm before claims are created.
- Step 9: the result in view right after Submit, failures not hidden behind a success toast, no way to submit twice.

## Checks (owner's rules, for every step)

- How will the user know it happened? Visible signal after every action (count, toast, row change, result).
- Could any button read as cancel, go back or did nothing? Watch "Back to selection", "Continue with POL only", the stepper.
- Confirm before commit, toast after? Policy choice: confirm and toast. Submit: should confirm too.
- Stay in context, no stumbles? Search is a dialog on the step (IMP-4); results must be in view.
- Same thing looks the same everywhere? Confirm dialogs, badges, policy tags, result banners.
- Plain language? No "AiP", "conclude", "impacted".
- Only existing NDBX or app components? nx-table, nx-checkbox, nx-badge, nx-message, app-confirm-dialog, app-empty-state, toast.
