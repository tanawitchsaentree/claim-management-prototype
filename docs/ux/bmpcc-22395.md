## Who

Claims manager (team lead) of a property claims team of about 6, up to 20 to 30 people (AC-01 note).
Still handles a few claims personally (AC-01 "My claims").
Knows the people, their strengths, who is on holiday, which clients are sensitive.
Does not know every claim's detail. Relies on the system to point at what is late or large.
Juggling: own claims, approvals, escalations from handlers, questions from their own boss about big losses, holiday cover.
How often: at the start of the day and a few times a day when a handler or broker escalates. (assumption)
Role risk: no Claims Manager role exists in GIAM yet (2866094791), so who sees this page is not settled.

## Story

As a claims manager, I want to see my team's open claims, who carries what, and which ones need me, so that I can spread the work fairly and step in on late or large claims before they go wrong.

Real-world triggers:
- Monday morning check: "what is burning in my team?"
- A handler goes on sick leave or holiday and their claims need a new owner.
- My boss asks about a large loss, and I need to find it and see who has it.
- A handler marks a claim "for review" and expects me to look.

## Journey

1. Opens the dashboard.
   Sees: welcome line, Claims Manager badge, five numbers, My claims, Team workload.
   Knows: roughly how the team was last week.
   Does: scans the numbers.
   Feels: wants one glance to tell "fine" or "trouble".

2. Picks a problem type.
   Sees: tiles Open team claims, Need attention, Overdue, High value, For review.
   Knows: what each word means to them, not necessarily to the system (thresholds are placeholders, CM-FLAG-1).
   Does: clicks a tile, for example Overdue.
   Feels: expects the list to show exactly that number of claims.

3. Reads the list.
   Sees: claim, client, handler, flag badges, last updated, loss amount.
   Knows: which clients matter.
   Does: hovers a badge for the reason, sorts by amount.
   Feels: confident if the list matches the number; uneasy if it does not.

4. Looks at a person.
   Sees: team table, one row per member plus Team queue.
   Knows: who is overloaded, who is away.
   Does: View claims on a member; later View whole team.
   Feels: wants to compare people quickly.

5. Acts on a claim.
   Sees: row menu with Open claim and Reassign.
   Knows: who has room.
   Does: Reassign, picks a handler, confirms; or opens the claim.
   Feels: careful, because it moves someone's work and notifies two people.

6. Comes back.
   Sees: the dashboard with the same filters (AC-11).
   Does: presses Back from the claim, carries on.
   Feels: should feel like they never left.

7. Done.
   Sees: numbers changed after the reassign, toast confirmed it.
   Feels: in control, knows the team was told.

## Pain points

- Numbers that do not match the list. A tile says 10, the list shows fewer, the manager thinks the data is wrong. (AC-03 "top 8 rows")
- Mixed currencies. CHF, EUR, USD, SGD in one list; "High value" is 250,000 in the claim currency with no FX (AC-02 accepted deviation). Sorting by amount compares raw numbers.
- Reassigning blind. Without load or availability per person, the manager may give work to someone already full or away. Capacity checks are a known business need (2835328245, 2237135667).
- Out of date data. The page loads once (CM-REFRESH-1); a manager may act on two-hour-old numbers.
- Losing place. Refresh, Back or a filter change that scrolls or blanks the page breaks the scan.
- Partial data that quietly lowers totals (AC-08).
- No role yet in GIAM, so "no permission" will be a real case for early users (2866094791).
- Flag reasons only on hover. A keyboard user or a quick scanner never sees why.

## Needs

- On landing: the five numbers, clear words, the time the data is from.
- After a tile click: the list in view, filtered, with a heading and a count that match the tile.
- In the list: why a claim is flagged, who has it, how big it is, in comparable terms.
- In the team table: load per person and whether they are available.
- Before reassign: who has room. After reassign: a toast and the row moving.
- On refresh: the page stays put, only the numbers update.
- In any empty or error state: what happened, and a way forward.

- Visible signal after every action: tile click, filter, sort, drill-down, refresh, reassign.

- Plain language, no system words.
