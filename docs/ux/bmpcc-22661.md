## Who

Claim handler on a commercial property claim. Example: Nina Feld on CLM-2024-001.
- Knows the claim, the sections, the reserves and payments she wants to make.
- Does not know Global Genius from the inside. It is "the finance books". She never logs in there.
- Is juggling 30 to 80 open claims, calls from brokers, payment deadlines.
- Sees this rarely. Maybe a few times a month across her whole portfolio (assumption). So she will not remember what to do from last time.
- Second persona: Finance colleague who gets told about the failure. Not on this screen, but the handler may quote the message to them.

## Story

As a claim handler, I want to see when the claim or one of its sections could not be booked in Global Genius, with Global Genius's own reason, and retry it myself, so that payments and reserves are not stuck and I can tell Finance exactly what went wrong.

Real-world trigger: she opens Claim Overview to pay a contractor or raise a reserve. Or the bell tells her "section X couldn't be booked". The backend has already retried 3 times on its own before she sees anything (Confluence 2984733393).

## Journey

1. Opens Claim Overview.
   - Sees: a red message at the top of the page.
   - Knows: something about "Global Genius" is wrong. Not sure if it is her fault.
   - Does: reads the first line.
   - Feels: worried. "Can I still pay? Did I break something?"
2. Reads what it means for her.
   - Sees: plain line "payments and reserves are on hold", then "Message from Global Genius: BKG-4102 ..." (AC-01).
   - Knows: money is blocked. The code looks technical.
   - Does: decides between retry now, or wait for Finance.
   - Feels: relieved that it says Finance have been told.
3. Presses Retry.
   - Sees: the message turns blue, "Retrying" (AC-03, AC-04).
   - Knows: her click was taken.
   - Does: waits a few seconds, may scroll away or open another page.
   - Feels: hopeful, slightly impatient.
4. Gets the answer.
   - Works: claim message turns green "now set up" (AC-01 outcome). Section message disappears, green toast, bell line under Done (AC-05).
   - Fails again: red again, "Retried, still not booked. You can retry once more", red toast, bell line (AC-03, AC-04).
   - Feels: done, or annoyed but informed.
5. Second failed retry.
   - Sees: "now with Finance", no Retry button (AC-06).
   - Knows: it is out of her hands.
   - Does: carries on with the rest of the claim, watches the bell.
   - Feels: OK if it is clear what still works and who owns it.
6. Claim and sections both failed.
   - Sees: only the claim message. Sections show up once the claim is set up (AC-07).
   - Feels: may be surprised that more red appears right after a success.

## Pain points

- PP1 Jargon. "Global Genius", "booking", "BKG-5021 ... line of business code" mean nothing to a handler. The plain line must come first (AC-01 decision GG-MSG-1).
- PP2 Did my click do anything? A retry takes a few seconds and the backend answers later. Without an instant change she clicks again or leaves (AC-04).
- PP3 Silent outcome. If she leaves the page, the answer must still reach her (toast, bell) (AC-04, AC-05).
- PP4 Same problem, different look. The claim message appears on Claim Overview and on Financial Overview. If they differ she cannot trust either (owner rule "same thing looks the same").
- PP5 Retrying forever. The backend already tried 3 times. After 2 more manual tries more clicks will not help (AC-06, ADR 2984733393).
- PP6 Wall of red. A claim with 3 or 4 failed sections stacks many red boxes above the claim details (assumption).
- PP7 What can I still do? She needs to know payments and reserves are on hold, and whether other work (notes, documents) is fine (assumption).
- PP8 Who may retry. Open point in the epic, not decided. Prototype lets everyone retry (CONVERSIONS.md GG-SEC-1).
- PP9 Closed claim. A failed booking on a closed or reopened claim is not described anywhere (assumption).

## Needs

- On landing: one clear line saying what is blocked, then Global Genius's text labelled as theirs, then what to do.
- On retry: an instant visible change on the same message, button gone so she cannot double-click.
- On outcome: the message itself changes, plus a toast, plus a bell line she can find later.
- On second failure: no button, a clear owner (Finance), and "we'll let you know".
- Everywhere the claim message shows, the same text, the same Global Genius message, the same working button.
- Section name readable, matching the Sections page.

## Checks (owner's rules, applied to every step)

- Visible signal after every action: Retry must change the message at once, and the result must show on the page, in a toast and in the bell.
- Could a button read as cancel or did nothing: "Retry booking" and "Retry setting up this claim in Global Genius" must never be a dead button.
- Confirm before commit, toast after: GG-SEC-1 skips the confirm on purpose (same event again, no money moves). The toast after is still required.
- Stay in context: retry from where she sees the problem, no page change.
- Same thing looks the same everywhere: claim message on Claim Overview equals the one on Financial Overview. Claim and section outcomes follow one pattern.
- Plain language: plain line first, Global Genius code second and labelled.
- Only existing NDBX and app components: nx-message, nxButton, app toast-stack, notification-panel.

### Problems

P1. Retry on Financial Overview does nothing (GG-CX-01).
- What the user sees: the same red "This claim isn't in Global Genius yet" message, without the "Message from Global Genius" line. She presses "Retry setting up this claim in Global Genius". Nothing changes, no Retrying, no toast, even after 4 seconds (CX01-2, CX01-3). Back on Claim Overview it is still failed, with the Global Genius text.
- Why it hurts: breaks "did nothing" and "same thing looks the same everywhere" (PP2, PP4). Financial Overview is exactly where a handler goes when a payment is blocked.
- Cause: src/app/features/claims/financial-overview/financial-overview.component.html:6 uses the banner without [claimId], so sendAgain() at gg-registration-banner.component.ts:79-81 sends for an empty claim id and failureMessage at :61-64 has no claim to read.
- Fix: pass the claim id the way Claim Overview does, src/app/features/claims/claim-overview/claim-overview.component.html:10. Same component, no new UI.

P2. Claim retry gives no toast, section retry does (GG-CL-02, GG-CL-03).
- What the user sees: after a section retry a green or red toast says how it went. After a claim retry there is no toast at all, success or failure. If she scrolled down or moved on, the claim result only shows in the bell on success, and nowhere on failure until she scrolls back up.
- Why it hurts: breaks "toast after" and "same thing looks the same" (PP3, PP4). The ticket walkthrough step 2 promises "a toast and the bell say how it went" for both.
- Fix: in resolveClaim (src/app/core/mock/services/mock-booking.service.ts:153-172) use the same toast calls as resolveSection, src/app/core/mock/services/mock-booking.service.ts:77 (success) and :86 (error).

P3. The failure toast points to the page she is already on.
- What the user sees: on Claim Overview, a red toast "Contents & Fixtures Damage still couldn't be booked. The message from Global Genius is on Claim Overview." (SC02-3).
- Why it hurts: reads as if she must go somewhere else. Small stumble in "stay in context" and plain language.
- Fix: say what changed instead, for example "You can retry once more" or "Finance have it now", the same lines as sectionBookingHint, src/app/core/models/gg-section-booking.model.ts:44-49. Keep the "on Claim Overview" pointer only when she is on another page, like the bell link in src/app/core/mock/services/mock-booking.service.ts:83.

P4. Ticket text no longer matches what is built (AC-02, AC-04, AC-06).
- What the user sees (tester): AC-02 says one red banner "2 sections couldn't be booked in Global Genius" with rows, AC-06 says "1 section still couldn't be booked". The screen shows one message per section titled with the section name (commit c42f091). AC-04 promises a spinner; the build shows a blue info message with no spinner.
- Why it hurts: whoever checks the ticket against the screen will report a failure that is not one, or trust a wrong description.
- Fix: update the plainStatement of AC-02, AC-04, AC-06 in public/tickets/bmpcc-22661.json to the one-message-per-section build. The Retrying look matches the claim one, src/app/shared/components/gg-registration-banner/gg-registration-banner.component.html:2, so it is the ticket that should change, not the screen.

P5. After a failed section retry the title stays the same.
- What the user sees: before and after the first failed retry the title reads "Contents & Fixtures Damage couldn't be booked in Global Genius". Only the last small line changes to "Retried, still not booked" (SC01-2 compared with SC02-3). The claim message changes its title ("Retried, but the claim still couldn't be set up").
- Why it hurts: "did my retry do anything?" once the toast has gone (PP2). Claim and section behave differently on one page (PP4).
- Fix: change the section title after one failed retry, like the claim title in src/app/core/models/gg-claim-registration.model.ts:37-44, inside messageFor at src/app/shared/components/gg-section-booking-banner/gg-section-booking-banner.component.ts:26-38.

P6. Wall of red, and red right after a success (GG-SC-01, GG-SQ-01).
- What the user sees: each failed section is a full red box with 4 lines and a button. Two sections push General information down by about 400 px. Right after "This claim is now set up" (green) two new red boxes appear under it (SQ01-2).
- Why it hurts: hides the claim details and makes a good moment feel like a new problem (PP6). With 4 sections this gets worse (assumption).
- Fix: when there is more than one failed section, show one red nx-message with a short list, the way financial warnings group items in src/app/shared/components/financial-warnings/financial-warnings.component.html (the .fw-list stack the banner already cites at gg-section-booking-banner.component.scss:1). For SQ01, add to the green message "2 sections still need booking, see below" so the red is expected.

P7. Section names show an em-dash (Property Damage, then a long dash, then Warehouse & Forklift).
- What the user sees: the dash in the title, the toast and the bell.
- Why it hurts: plain-language rule, and it differs from the section name elsewhere. Known seed data quirk (CONVERSIONS.md 2026-10-20 "stray dash in seeded section names").
- Fix: clean the seed section names in src/app/core/mock/data (then run npm run audit:ac-logic).

P8. Not built: who may retry, and closed claims (GG-PM-01, GG-CC-01).
- What the user sees: anyone with the claim open gets Retry. A closed claim has no defined behaviour.
- Why it hurts: PP8, PP9. A user without rights would press a button that later fails on the server.
- Fix: needs a product decision first. If retry is restricted, hide the button and say who can retry, the same way "now with Finance" already hides it (gg-section-booking.model.ts:39-41 and gg-claim-registration.model.ts:29-35).
