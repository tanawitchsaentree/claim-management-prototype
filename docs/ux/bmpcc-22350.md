# BMPCC-22350 Large Loss Heads-Up

Where it lives: the "Large loss Heads-Up" card on Claim Overview, the bell, and the full page /claims/:id/heads-up.

Sources used
- Ticket: public/tickets/bmpcc-22350.json, AC-01 to AC-11 and walkthroughSteps.
- Confluence 2761627833 "Large Loss Heads-Up in CLAWS" (the legacy CLAWS training page: 48 hour rule, 1M warning, 5M block, link to an existing Heads-Up, Global Claims review, reassign).
- Confluence 3349387316 heads-up-flow.drawio exists (not opened, diagram file).
- Production: ../claims-management-main has no Heads-Up code at all (grep for heads-up / large loss found nothing). So the new product has no shipped behaviour to copy; CLAWS is the only real-world reference.
- Built code: features/claims/heads-up/**, claim-overview/components/heads-up-card/**, shared/components/create-heads-up-dialog/**, core/mock/services/mock-large-loss-heads-up.service.ts.

## Who

Main persona: the claim handler who owns the claim.
- Knows the claim well: the loss, the policy, the latest reserve, who the broker is.
- Does not always know the Key Case Management (KCM) rules by heart. The thresholds and the reason list are business vocabulary (OE, LOE, IIP, ART share, AZRe).
- Is juggling many open claims, reserve changes, payments and broker calls at the same time.
- Does this rarely. A handler may write a few Heads-Ups a year. So the screen must teach, not assume.
- Under time pressure: CLAWS says the Heads-Up must go out within 48 hours of the loss becoming known (Confluence 2761627833, section 1).

Second persona: the KCM / Global Claims reviewer (Klaus in the prototype).
- Reads the submitted Heads-Up. In CLAWS can only edit after assigning it to themselves (Confluence 2761627833, section 4).
- In this ticket the KCM side is out of scope (walkthroughSteps line 4). Only the read side matters: what they receive must be complete and trustworthy.

Third persona (assumption): the claims manager, who in CLAWS often starts the request and asks the handler to write it.

## Story

As a claim handler, I want to tell Key Case Management early about a loss that is or may become large, so that they can step in, inform the Group and set the strategy before it surprises anyone.

Real-world triggers
- The incurred on the claim crosses 1M. The system makes a draft for the handler (AC-01).
- The claim is still small but something makes it sensitive: press coverage, public interest, Board attention, a D&O class action (AC-03, reason list in core/models/large-loss-heads-up.model.ts:42).
- A Heads-Up already exists and the amount crosses 1M later. No second one, only a notice (AC-02).

## Journey

1. Trigger arrives
- Sees: bell badge "1 notifications need you", line "CLM-...: incurred reached 1M, a draft Heads-Up is ready for you to complete" (AC-01). Or nothing, if it is a manual case.
- Knows/believes: "Something about a big claim needs me. Is it urgent? By when?"
- Does: opens the bell line, or opens the claim.
- Feels: alert, a bit of pressure.

2. On Claim Overview
- Sees: the Large loss Heads-Up card. Either "No Heads-Up for this claim. Incurred is 125,000 EUR" with Create Heads-Up (AC-03), or a Draft chip with the blue threshold note and Continue Heads-Up (AC-01/02), or Submitted with View Heads-Up (AC-10).
- Knows: the state of the Heads-Up in one glance, if the card is visible.
- Does: presses Create / Continue / View.
- Feels: oriented, if the status is clear.

3. Creating by hand (only below 1M or with no draft)
- Sees: dialog "Create a Heads-Up", 8 reasons with one-line hints, optional note.
- Knows: why they are doing it; may not know the exact reason label.
- Does: ticks one or more reasons, presses Create draft Heads-Up. Nothing ticked gives "Tick at least one reason".
- Feels: guided, if the hints are clear.

4. Checking what came from the claim
- Sees: "From the claim" in Loss, Policy, Financial data. Each value says "From the claim", "Changed by you. Claim value: X" with Use the claim value, "Not on the claim yet. Enter it yourself", or "Waiting for Finance" (AC-04, AC-06, AC-07).
- Knows: which values they can trust and which they must type.
- Does: corrects what is wrong, fills gaps.
- Feels: in control, as long as the source labels are honest.

5. Writing the assessment
- Sees: Cat event and Multiple LOBs affected (Yes/No), four text boxes with hints and 0/4000 counters (AC-05).
- Does: writes, may stop halfway and save a draft.
- Feels: this is the real work; fear of losing text.

6. Adding attachments
- Sees: claim documents to tick, Choose files, failed uploads with Try again (AC-09).
- Does: picks the adjuster report, uploads the fire brigade report.
- Feels: wants to see each file land.

7. Saving a draft
- Sees: confirm "Save this Heads-Up as a draft?", then toast "Draft saved" and "Last saved ..." under the title (AC-08).
- On failure: red message "Couldn't save the Heads-Up. Your changes are still here." and a red toast.
- Feels: safe to leave.

8. Submitting
- Sees: if gaps, a red summary at the top and an error per field (AC-05). If complete, confirm "Submit this Heads-Up to Key Case Management?" with Keep editing.
- Then: toast "Heads-Up submitted", the page turns read-only with a green note (AC-10).
- Feels: relief; needs proof it went.

9. Afterwards
- Sees: card on Claim Overview says Submitted, by whom and when, View Heads-Up.
- Knows: KCM has it, and later news goes to KCM directly.

## Pain points

- The 48 hour rule (Confluence 2761627833) is nowhere on the screen. A draft made by the system can sit forgotten once the bell line is read.
- Business labels (OE, LOE for IIP, ART share, AZRe exposure) are not explained (AC-04 decision keeps them on purpose).
- Pre-filled values can be stale or wrong. The handler must see where each one came from (AC-04, AC-07).
- Finance figures may not have arrived yet (AC-06). The handler may think the form is broken.
- Long free texts (up to 4000 characters each). Losing them by navigating away is the worst outcome.
- Saving can fail (AC-08). Must not lose work.
- Uploads can fail, be too big or the wrong type (AC-09).
- Submit is final (AC-10 decision). "Submit" must not be mistaken for "save".
- In CLAWS a manager or a second person often reviews before submit (Confluence 2761627833, section 7). The prototype has no review or reassign step. Assumption: accepted for this ticket.
- Closed claims: a Heads-Up on a closed claim makes little sense; the card hides Create (heads-up-card.component.ts:52).
- Who may edit: CLAWS restricts editing to the assigned person. The prototype does not say.

## Needs

- At the trigger: what happened, which claim, what to do, ideally by when.
- On the card: status chip, who and when, one clear next action.
- In the dialog: short reason hints, a clear error if nothing is ticked, Cancel that really cancels.
- On the form: source of each value, a way back to the claim value, what is still needed before submit.
- On every save: confirm first, toast after, the "Last saved" line updated.
- On leave: a warning before unsaved text is lost, whatever way they leave.
- After submit: a clear "it went, it is locked" message on the form and on the card.

## Checks

Applied to every step of the journey.
- Visible signal after every action: toast or status change on create, save, submit, upload, remove, reset.
- No button that could read as cancel, go back or did nothing.
- Confirm before commit, toast after.
- Stay in context: form is a full page like Make payment, back goes to the claim; no dead ends.
- Same thing looks the same everywhere: status chip on card and page, footer like Make payment, attachments like the attachment picker.
- Plain language.
- Only existing NDBX / app components.

## Audit 2026-11-17

How it was walked: dev server on port 4261, Playwright at 1440x900 (and 760 wide for HU-RS-01), persona Mara (claim handler), each scenario loaded with the DEV banner ticket BMPCC-22350 and its AC. Screenshots in /tmp/ux-audit/22350-<scenario>-<step>.png. No console errors seen.

Result: 18 scenarios, 12 pass, 6 fail.

### HU-CR-01 Create by hand: pass, with one problem
Card, dialog, "Tick at least one reason", toast and pre-filled form all work. Screenshots 22350-HU-CR-01-1 to 4.
Problem: mixed message after create
- What the user sees: toast "Draft Heads-Up created", while the line under the title says "Started ... by Mara Mustermann, not saved yet". The card on Claim Overview already shows it as a Draft.
- Why it hurts: check "how will the user know it happened". Was it created or not? A handler may press Save draft straight away just to be sure, or worry it is lost.
- Fix: say "Created 17-11-2026 11:13 by Mara Mustermann, not submitted yet" in the status line (heads-up-page.component.html:44), same pattern as the "Last saved ... by ..." line in the same block.
Minor: the toast sits over the sticky footer, between Save draft and Submit (22350-HU-CR-01-4). The toast stack is fixed bottom centre for the whole app (shared/components/toast/toast-stack.component.scss:2-6), so this is systemic, not a Heads-Up fix.

### HU-CR-02 Cancel the create dialog: pass
Cancel and the X close the dialog; the card still says "No Heads-Up for this claim". 22350-HU-CR-02-1.

### HU-TH-01 Incurred reaches 1M: pass, with one problem
Bell line, card note, form note all as AC-01; the bell line is gone after opening it once. 22350-HU-TH-01-1 to 5.
Problem: no deadline, nothing after the bell
- What the user sees: "a draft Heads-Up was made for you. Complete it and submit it to Key Case Management." No "by when". Once the bell line is read, the only trace is the card, third card down on Claim Overview.
- Why it hurts: pain point "48 hour rule" (Confluence 2761627833). A system-made draft can be forgotten. Need "at the trigger: by when".
- Fix: add the due time to the existing note in heads-up-card.component.html (nx-message context="info") and keep the Draft visible in the handler's task list. Assumption: needs product sign-off on whether the 48 hours apply to the new system.
Minor wording: "Continue Heads-Up" on a draft the handler never opened reads as if they had started it. "Complete Heads-Up" matches the note text.

### HU-TH-02 Heads-Up already exists: pass
Notice on card and form, same draft, "Last saved 11-11-2026 22:40". 22350-HU-TH-02-1, 2.

### HU-PF-01 Pre-filled values: pass
"From the claim" under every value, Cause of loss shows the reset button, reset brings back "Fire". 22350-HU-PF-01-1, 2.
Minor: reset gives no toast; the hint changing back is the signal. Acceptable.

### HU-PF-02 Partial pre-fill: pass

### HU-PF-03 Finance pending: pass
Blue note under Financial data, 7 inputs say "Waiting for Finance". 22350-HU-PF-03-1.

### HU-SB-01 Submit with gaps, then submit: pass
Red summary "Fill in 5 fields before you submit", per-field errors, the summary empties as fields are filled, confirm with "Keep editing", toast "Heads-Up submitted", page turns read-only, card says Submitted. 22350-HU-SB-01-1 to 6.
Minor: after submit the page tries to scroll up, but the screenshot is still mid-form; the green "Key Case Management has this Heads-Up" note at the top is out of view. The toast covers it for now.

### HU-SV-01 Save draft: fail (small)
Confirm, toast "Draft saved", "Last saved 17-11-2026 11:16", card updated. 22350-HU-SV-01-1, 2.
Problem: the Save draft confirm's second button is "Cancel"
- What the user sees: "Save this Heads-Up as a draft?" with Cancel and Save draft. The Submit confirm and the Leave confirm both say "Keep editing".
- Why it hurts: check "could any button read as cancel" and "same thing looks the same". "Cancel" on a Heads-Up page can read as "cancel the Heads-Up", and the page footer also has a Cancel that means leave.
- Fix: cancelLabel "Keep editing" in confirmSave draft branch (heads-up-confirm.ts:8-15), same as the submit branch at heads-up-confirm.ts:21.

### HU-SV-02 Save fails: pass
Red message and red toast, text kept, second try saves with green toast. 22350-HU-SV-02-1 to 3.

### HU-AT-01 Attachments: pass
Try again works, a PDF lands in the list, "bad.exe: this file type can't be attached", "big.pdf is over 25 MB", remove with the X. 22350-HU-AT-01-1 to 6.
Minor: remove has no undo and no toast, only a screen reader message. One misclick removes a file. The claim-document rows are tick boxes, so those come back easily; uploaded files do not.

### HU-RO-01 Submitted, read-only: pass
Green note, values as text, "Changed. Claim value: Fire", no footer, card "Submitted ... View Heads-Up". 22350-HU-RO-01-1, 2.

### HU-LV-01 Leave with unsaved changes: fail
Cancel shows "Leave without saving?" with Keep editing, and the text is kept. 22350-HU-LV-01-1.
Problem: leaving any other way loses the text silently
- What the user sees: after typing in Loss, clicking Claim overview in the side menu (or any header link, or the browser back) goes straight to the claim. No warning. The text is gone. 22350-HU-LV-01-2.
- Why it hurts: pain point "losing 4000 characters of assessment is the worst outcome"; need "a warning before unsaved text is lost, whatever way they leave". The side menu is always on screen next to the form.
- Fix: add a canDeactivate guard on the claims/:id/heads-up route calling the existing confirmLeave(), copying editLossInformationCanDeactivate (app.routes.ts:97, features/claims/edit-loss-information/edit-loss-information.guard.ts:10, confirmLeaveIfDirty at edit-loss-information.component.ts:795).

### HU-CL-01 Closed claim: fail
No Heads-Up on a closed claim: the card and the page hide Create. 22350-HU-CL-01-1, 2.
Problem 1: no reason given
- What the user sees: "No Heads-Up for this claim. Incurred is 125,000 EUR." and no button, no word about why.
- Why it hurts: check "visible signal" and "no stumbles"; the handler may think the feature is broken.
- Fix: add "The claim is closed, so a Heads-Up can't be started." in the card text, same wording family as financial-overview.component.ts:146 ("Claim is closed").
Problem 2: a draft on a closed claim stays fully editable
- What the user sees: an existing draft on a closed claim opens with inputs, Save draft and Submit Heads-Up, and no note that the claim is closed. 22350-HU-CL-01-3.
- Why it hurts: KCM can get a referral on a claim that is already closed; the card and the page disagree (card blocks Create, page allows Submit).
- Fix: treat Closed like Submitted for the footer (readonly at heads-up-page.component.ts:95) and show an nx-message, the way Sections disables actions with claimClosed() (features/sections/sections.html:234, sections.ts:229). Assumption: product decides whether a draft on a closed claim may still be submitted.

### HU-PM-01 Someone else opens the draft: fail (assumption)
- What the user sees: switching to the KCM persona (Klaus), the handler's draft opens with Save draft and Submit Heads-Up. Nothing says whose draft it is beyond "Started by Mara Mustermann". 22350-HU-PM-01-1.
- Why it hurts: CLAWS only lets the assigned person edit (Confluence 2761627833, section 4). Two people can overwrite each other's text; a KCM user could submit a referral to themselves.
- Fix: show the read-only view (the existing submitted layout in heads-up-prefill-section.component.html, readonly branch) for anyone who is not the claim's handler, with an nx-message saying who owns it. Needs a product decision on roles.

### HU-ER-01 Claim can't be loaded: fail (small)
- What the user sees: for an unknown claim id, "Couldn't load this claim. Check your connection and try again." with Try again. 22350-HU-ER-01-1.
- Why it hurts: plain language and honesty. The cause is a wrong claim, not the connection; Try again can never work, so it reads as "did nothing".
- Fix: split "not found" from "network error" and use app-empty-state (shared/components/empty-state/empty-state.component.html), as the page already does for "This claim has no Heads-Up yet" (heads-up-page.component.html, empty-state branch), with a Back to claim search action. Note: only reachable by a typed or old link.

### HU-RS-01 Narrow window: pass
At 760 wide the grid drops to two columns, footer stays usable. 22350-HU-RS-01-1. German text length not reviewed (AC-11 says partial).

### HU-DB-01 Finding drafts outside the claim: fail (gap, out of ticket scope)
- What the user sees: the handler dashboard has no Heads-Up entry; the "Heads-up: Monitored Claims" widget is KCM only (walkthroughSteps line 4). 22350-HU-DB-01-1.
- Why it hurts: once the bell line is read, a draft is only findable by opening the right claim. Ties to the 48 hour pain point.
- Fix: list open Heads-Up drafts in the handler's existing task or "needs you" surfaces (the bell panel, shared/components/notification-panel/notification-panel.component.html:6) until submitted, instead of "told once". Product call.

### Note for testers (not a user problem)
Applying a BMPCC-22350 scenario in the DEV banner while already on the Heads-Up page leaves the pre-fill empty, because the page is not reloaded. Open the scenario from Claim Overview (or another claim) first.
