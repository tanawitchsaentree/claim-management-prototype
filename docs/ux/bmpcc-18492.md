## Who

Claim handler on commercial property and business interruption claims (KCM / claim handler role).
Knows the client, the policy and the loss story from calls and broker mails.
Does not always know the CBI case-type names by heart. They come from the policy wording.
Juggling many open claims, reserves, broker chasing and section reviews at once.
How often: rarely per claim, usually once or twice. Typical moments are a few days after FNOL when the facts come in, or when the broker corrects a supplier name. (assumption, based on the FD saying CBI used to be captured only at Final Payment)

Secondary: the underwriting and reporting side reads this data later via Kafka (FD 3163290837 "Downstream"). They never see this screen but rely on it being right.

## Story

As a claim handler, I want to record or correct whether a Business Interruption loss came from damage at a supplier, customer or other third party, and who and where that was, so that the right CBI section and limits apply and the group can track CBI accumulation.

Real-world trigger:
- The broker tells me the outage was caused by a fire at the client's chip supplier, after the claim was already registered.
- Or the supplier name I typed at FNOL was wrong.
- Or Business interruption gets added or removed on the claim, and the CBI question appears or goes away with it.

## Journey

1. Trigger: new facts arrive.
   Sees: mail or call from broker or client.
   Knows: "the loss was really caused at the supplier".
   Does: opens the claim, goes to Claim overview.
   Feels: in a hurry, wants a quick fix.

2. Finds the place to change it.
   Sees: Claim overview, General information card, "Edit claim details" link.
   Knows: CBI is part of the loss facts, so expects it under claim details. (assumption)
   Does: clicks Edit claim details.
   Feels: unsure if CBI lives here or in Sections.

3. Reads the current answers.
   Sees: Loss information card, then the CBI card with its answers, or "Not answered yet" with a reason.
   Knows: what was captured at FNOL.
   Does: scans to check what is wrong.
   Feels: oriented, if the labels match their words.

4. Opens the CBI card.
   Sees: Edit (or Answer) on the card. After pressing, the same questions as FNOL.
   Does: picks Yes / No, the case type from one grouped list, fills name, industry, location, authority order or utility type depending on the type.
   Feels: fine if the list is readable; confused if the case-type names are jargon.

5. Adds or changes the originating location.
   Sees: Add location, a modal with Country, City, ZIP required.
   Does: types the supplier site address, adds it.
   Feels: ok if the result shows clearly in the card.

6. Presses Update.
   Sees: either a refusal with the missing fields marked, or a confirm with Field / Original / Updated.
   Knows: what will change, and whether sections are affected ("Review needed").
   Does: checks the rows, presses Confirm changes or Back to editing.
   Feels: safe, because nothing is saved without the confirm.

7. Done.
   Sees: a toast "Claim details updated", the card back in read view with the new values.
   Knows: it saved, and whether sections need a review.
   Does: goes back to the claim or to Sections.
   Feels: done, if the change is visible where they look next.

## Pain points

- The handler does not know CBI is missing unless they open Edit claim details. (AC-02 only shows the prompt on this page)
- 12 case types with long technical names, 4 of them "Denial of Access, Without PD Trigger (...)". Easy to pick the wrong one. (FD 3163290837 "The 12 CBI Case Types")
- Fields change per case type. Switching type silently drops answers the handler typed. (AC-04)
- Words differ between places: "Caused by CBI", "Contingent business interruption", "CBI third party", "Supplier name" all mean pieces of the same thing.
- Answering CBI changes which sections should exist (FD "Section Creation Logic": BI only + Yes means CBI section replaces the BI section). The handler needs to know if sections will change, and they should not be warned when nothing changes (BI + No is "unchanged").
- Post-registration CBI editing depends on BMPCC-14960 (Edit Claim AiP isolation), not built yet. The FD says it "cannot work correctly until BMPCC-14960 lands". So the prototype promises behaviour the backend cannot deliver yet. (FD 3163290837 "Post-Registration")
- Location: legacy ABS has one CBI location per claim with Edit and Remove; mandatory Country, City, ZIP (BR 3234279112).
- Loss of Attraction needs a "Trigger event location" per the FD, still an open point. (FD "Category-specific fields")
- Closed claims should not be editable.

## Needs

- At the claim level: a visible hint when CBI is required but unanswered.
- In read view: plain labels that match the confirm table and the toast word for word.
- When editing: only the fields that apply, required ones marked, grouped case-type list.
- When switching case type: a clear sign that some answers will be cleared (the confirm shows it).
- On Update: refusal that points to each missing field, or a confirm listing every change.
- Section impact said before commit ("Review needed"), and only when it is true.
- After commit: toast plus the new values in place.
- Leaving with unsaved changes: a discard warning.

## Checks (owner's rules, applied to every step)

- Visible signal after every action: Edit opens the form, "Edited" tag, Update (n) count, refusal message, confirm, toast, read view.
- No button that reads as cancel or did nothing: card Cancel vs footer Discard vs modal Back to editing.
- Confirm before commit (Field / Original / Updated), toast after.
- Stay in context: everything on one page, no jump to Sections unless asked.
- Same thing looks the same: same labels in read view, edit view, confirm, toast; same location table everywhere; same error wording.
- Plain language: case-type names and labels a handler would say.
- Only existing NDBX / app components.

## Audit 2026-11-17

Walked with Playwright on localhost:4261, scenarios seeded with the ticket's own stateOverrides through the DEV banner helper. Screenshots in /tmp/ux-audit/18492-*.png. No console errors.

### CBI-RD-01 Read answered CBI: pass

### CBI-ED-01 Change supplier name: fail
Update (1), confirm, toast "Claim details updated", card shows new name. Screenshots 18492-ED-01-1 to -6.

Problem 1. Same field, three names.
- What the user sees: the card says "Supplier name", the confirm table says "CBI third party", the toast says "CBI third party saved."
- Why it hurts: Check "same thing looks the same". The handler has to work out that the row in the confirm is the field they just typed in.
- Fix: run the confirm and toast labels through the same cbiReadLabel() the card already uses (src/app/features/claims/edit-loss-information/cbi-edit.ts:77). The comment at cbi-edit.ts:55 says the diff keeps a stable label on purpose; keep the key stable, show the read label.

Problem 2. The promised section update is invisible.
- What the user sees: AC-01 says the open Business Interruption section's CBI entities get the new name. On Sections, neither the old nor the new supplier name appears anywhere.
- Why it hurts: Need "change visible where they look next". syncCbiToSections (src/app/core/services/loss-info-sync.service.ts:76) only patches entities flagged isContingentBi, and the seeded section SEC-C-CLM-2024-008-1 has none. So the AC can't be demoed.
- Fix: seed a CBI entity on that section in core/mock/data/sections.json, the same shape the FNOL submit creates (then run npm run audit:ac-logic). Also note the FD says real post-registration CBI editing waits for BMPCC-14960.

### CBI-NO-01 First answer No: fail
Problem 6. A false "Review needed".
- What the user sees: answering No on a BI claim says "Sections were built from the old values, so the open ones will be marked Review needed", and the toast says 1 section was marked.
- Why it hurts: Pain point "only warn when true". The FD (3163290837, Section Creation Logic) says BI + No is the standard flow, sections unchanged. A handler learns to ignore Review needed when it fires for nothing.
- Fix: flag only when sections would change (Yes, or a case-type change). Prior art for a condition-based check sits in the same service, willCreateFirstSection (src/app/core/services/loss-info-sync.service.ts:96).
