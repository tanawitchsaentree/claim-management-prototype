# BMPCC-22635 Generate the FNOL Acknowledgement

UX basis for the FNOL Acknowledgement panel on the FNOL confirmation screen and on Claim Overview.
Written 2026-11-17. Audit at the bottom.

## Sources

- Ticket ACs: public/tickets/bmpcc-22635.json (AC-01 to AC-07), epic BMPCC-22600.
- Confluence 2940829713 "Use-Cases for AOMS in Commercial Claims": the handler triggers "Generate FNOL Acknowledgement" after FNOL is registered. AppShell calls AOMS. Screen: FNOL Summary. Delivery channel: Email. Recipient: TBD.
- Confluence 866923837 "(CL01) Claims Letter - FNOL Acknowledgement" (UK, older): the handler picks the recipient (customer, broker, claimant). Sent by email or post depending on the recipient's preference. Has a loss adjuster FAQ attachment.

## Who

Claim handler (Commercial Claims, for example Mara Mustermann).
- Knows the claim well: they just registered it, or they own it.
- Knows the policyholder or broker expects a quick "we got your claim".
- Is juggling many new claims, reserves, Heads-Ups, phone calls and emails at once.
- Does this once per new claim, so many times a week. It should feel routine and fast.
- Sometimes a second time, after details were corrected (AC-07).

Claims manager or approver.
- Opens the claim to check it, not to send letters.
- Assumption: should see whether the letter exists, but may not need to generate it.

## Story

As a claim handler, I want to generate the FNOL acknowledgement right after I register the claim, so that I can tell the policyholder or broker quickly that we received it and give them the claim reference.

Real-world trigger: the claim was just opened (FNOL Submit), or the handler opens a fresh claim on Claim Overview and sees the letter was not made yet. A second trigger: claim details were corrected and the letter must match.

## Journey

1. FNOL submitted.
   - Sees: green "1 claim opened: CL-..." and the FNOL acknowledgement panel under it (AC-02).
   - Believes: the claim exists now. Next job is to tell the customer.
   - Does: presses Generate FNOL acknowledgement.
   - Feels: wants it done in one go, worried "generate" might email the customer straight away.
2. Confirm.
   - Sees: a short confirm saying the letter uses the details as they are now and nothing is sent (AC-01).
   - Believes: safe, nothing leaves the building yet.
   - Does: presses Generate.
   - Feels: reassured.
3. Waiting.
   - Sees: spinner line and "Generating…" button, with "you can leave this page" (AC-04).
   - Believes: it is running in the background.
   - Does: goes to the claim or another task.
   - Feels: fine if it is clearly still running; anxious if it looks stuck.
4. Ready.
   - Sees: green toast, bell item under Done, the letter name as a link with when and by whom (AC-01, AC-05).
   - Believes: the letter exists on the claim.
   - Does: opens it to check the claim reference, dates, insured, policy number.
   - Feels: done with step one, now wants to send it.
5. Send.
   - Sees: assumption, needs a way to get the letter to the policyholder or broker (download, or attach to an email).
   - Believes: "Nothing is sent: you send the letter yourself" means the app helps them send it.
   - Does: looks for Download or for the letter in the claim's attachments.
   - Feels: stuck if there is no way out.
6. Later, details corrected.
   - Sees: Generate again, with a confirm saying the earlier letter stays (AC-07).
   - Does: generates a new version.
   - Feels: wants to see which letter is current and that the old one is still there for the record.

## Pain points

- "Generate" can be read as "send to customer" (AC-01 decision).
- The letter freezes the data as it is now. If details are wrong, the wrong letter goes out (AC-01, AC-07).
- The document service can be slow or fail (AC-06). Handler may leave and never find out.
- Orphan claim without policy: the letter cannot be made. A disabled button with no reason looks broken (AC-03).
- Closed or abandoned claim: generating makes no sense (AC-03).
- Several claims opened at once: which letter belongs to which claim (AC-02).
- After generating, the handler still has to send it. Recipient and channel are TBD in Confluence 2940829713. Old UK design lets the handler pick the recipient (866923837).
- Old versions: auditors and colleagues need to see which letter went out (AC-07 "the earlier letter is kept").

## Needs

- Before: what the letter is, that generating does not send it, and why it is not available if it is not.
- At confirm: plain words, no surprise sending.
- While running: a visible running state that survives leaving the page.
- After: a toast, a bell item, the letter on the card with when and who.
- To finish the job: a way to download or attach the letter, and see it in the claim's documents.
- On repeat: which letter is current, all earlier ones kept.
- At a glance on Claim Overview: is the letter done or not, same as sibling cards.

## Checks

- Visible signal after every action: panel change, toast, bell line after generate. Toast after failure.
- Could any button read as cancel, go back or "did nothing": "Generate" must not read as "send". "Generate again" must not look like the next required step.
- Confirm before commit, toast after: yes for generate and generate again. Try again needs no second confirm (AC-06).
- Stay in context: generate where the handler already is (FNOL screen, Claim Overview). No detour to a separate page.
- Same thing looks the same everywhere: one panel in both places (FNOL-ACK-1). Should match sibling Claim Overview cards like Heads-Up.
- Plain language: no "AOMS", no "trigger".
- Only existing NDBX and app components: nxButton, nx-message, nx-spinner, ConfirmDialogComponent, attachment-picker rows, status chip.

## Audit 2026-11-17

Walked with Playwright on http://localhost:4261 as Mara Mustermann. Scenarios loaded with the DEV banner (BMPCC-22635, AC-04 to AC-07) or by patching the claim's state. Screenshots in /tmp/ux-audit/22635-<scenario>-<step>.png.

Result: 16 scenarios. 10 pass, 3 fail, 3 not walked (1 not built, 2 checked in code only).

### ACK-OV-01 Pass

Panel under General information, confirm, spinner line, toast, bell under Done, letter link with "Generated 17-11-2026 11:14 by Mara Mustermann", letter opens. Shots OV-01-1 to OV-01-5, OV-01-bell.

Problem: the card has no status in its header.
- User sees: the title "FNOL acknowledgement" with no done or not-done marker. The Heads-Up card right below has a Draft / Submitted chip.
- Why it hurts: "same thing looks the same everywhere"; the need "at a glance, is it done".
- Fix: add app-status-chip in the card header, as in src/app/features/claims/claim-overview/components/heads-up-card/heads-up-card.component.html:5-9 ("Not generated" / "Generated").

Problem: the "Generated ... by ..." line sits at the far right edge, far from the file name.
- User sees: the link on the left, the date on the right side of the card, a wide empty gap between them.
- Why it hurts: eye has to jump across the card to connect who and when to the file.
- Fix: put the meta right after the name like the attachment rows in src/app/features/claims/provider-management/components/attachment-picker/attachment-picker.component.html:6-14.

### ACK-OV-05 Fail

Generate again confirm and v2 work: v2 shows on top, v1 below as "Earlier letter". Shots OV-05-1 to OV-05-3.

Problem: the first letter disappears after the third generation.
- User sees: after v3 only "v3" and "v2 Earlier letter". v1 is gone. Shot OV-05-4.
- Why it hurts: the confirm promises "The earlier letter stays on the claim" (AC-07). If v1 was the one sent, nobody can see it any more. Breaks the pain point "which letter went out".
- Fix: keep a list of all versions (model holds only one "previous", src/app/core/models/fnol-acknowledgement.model.ts:41) and show them as attachment rows, attachment-picker.component.html:4-16. Older versions could collapse under "Earlier letters (n)".

### ACK-OV-08 Pass

SK-2024-001: greyed button with "Link a policy first: the letter needs the policy details." beside it. Shot OV-08-1.

- Why it hurts: "same thing looks the same everywhere".
- Fix: pick one rule for closed claims across Claim Overview cards. Heads-Up uses canCreate to hide (heads-up-card.component.ts:52, html:67-76). Either both hide, or both disable with the reason.

### ACK-FN-02 Fail

Problem: no FNOL acknowledgement panel after the Policies step.
- User sees: "2 claims created" with a table of policies and claim links. No way to generate either letter here. Shot FN-02-1.
- Why it hurts: AC-02 "Several claims opened at once: one panel per claim". The handler loses the moment where they planned to do it and has to open each claim. "Stay in context".
- Fix: render the same panel per created claim under the result table, exactly as the single-claim path does in src/app/features/fnol/steps/step-summary/step-summary.component.html:21-23 (with showClaimId). The per-policy branch at step-summary.component.html:7-12 skips it.

Note outside this ticket: this screen also showed no Go to claim button or footer after submit.

### ACK-FN-03 Not walked

Could not seed several claim groups without the Policies step. Code renders one panel per claim with the claim number in the title (step-summary.component.html:21-23, panel html:3).

### ACK-FN-04 Checked in code only

Orphan FNOL ends on the skeleton summary, which has no panel. Claim Overview of the orphan claim shows the disabled panel with the reason (ACK-OV-08). Acceptable, assumption: no need to show a disabled panel on the skeleton summary.

### ACK-XX-01 Fail

Problem: after generating, the handler has no way to send the letter.
- User sees: the confirm says "Nothing is sent: you send the letter yourself". The letter dialog has only Close (shot OV-04-2). No Download. The letter is not added to the claim's documents, so the attachment picker ("From claim documents") cannot find it when writing to the broker.
- Why it hurts: the real job is telling the policyholder or broker (Story, Journey step 5). Confluence 2940829713 says the channel is email. The flow stops one step short and leaves the handler stuck.
- Fix: add the generated letter to the claim documents (MockClaimDocumentsService, src/app/core/mock/services/mock-claim-documents.service.ts:11) so it shows in the Attachments panel and in attachment-picker.component.html:2-16. Add a secondary Download button next to Close in the letter dialog footer (fnol-acknowledgement-preview.component.html footer).

Open question for the team: who is the recipient (policyholder or broker)? The panel text says "policyholder or broker" but the letter always starts "Dear <insured>". Confluence 866923837 lets the handler choose. Recipient is TBD in 2940829713.
