# BMPCC-22666 Manual reassignment of claims

UX basis written 2026-11-17, before the audit at the bottom.
Sources: ticket `public/tickets/bmpcc-22666.json` (AC-01 to AC-12), Confluence pages 3302433795, 3264392659, 3366776946, production source in `../claims-management-main`.
Anything marked "assumption" is my inference, not a stated requirement.

## Who

### Mara, claim handler (main persona)
- Handles 10 to 30 open commercial property claims at once (assumption, from the seed data and the Claims Manager dashboard counts).
- Knows her own claims well. Knows her team by name. Does not know other teams' managers by heart.
- Juggling: calls with brokers, reserves, payments, deadlines. Reassigning is not her job, it is an interruption.
- How often: a single claim now and then (needs a specialist, conflict of interest). A batch a few times a year, before leave.

### Tobias, claims manager
- Leads the Property claims team. Watches workload on the manager dashboard.
- Moves claims between people when someone is overloaded, off sick, or a claim is above their authority.
- How often: several times a week (assumption).

### Who it lands on
- Lena (receiving handler) and Nina (losing handler). They did not start the action, so they rely only on what the system tells them.

## Story

- As a claim handler, I want to hand a claim to a colleague or a team queue, so that it is handled by someone with the right skill, authority or time.
- As a claim handler, I want to take a claim from a colleague in my team, so that I can cover for them without waiting for a manager.
- As a handler registering a new claim, I want to send it straight to the right person, so that it does not sit with me first.
- As a claims manager, I want to move claims between my people, so that workload stays balanced.

Real-world triggers:
- A claim turns out to need a specialist (cyber, marine, large loss).
- The loss estimate grows past the handler's authority.
- The handler goes on leave or is off sick.
- A colleague is absent and the client is calling.
- Belgium today: team leaders reassign by hand through a reassignment dashboard, and routing is often wrong (Confluence 3264392659, "Business Context").
- Bulk transfer for absent colleagues is a confirmed need (Confluence 3264392659, decision OP-1395).

## Journey

### A. Reassign my own claim (Claim Overview)
1. Trigger. Mara reads the claim and realises it needs someone else.
   - Sees: Claim Overview, General information, "Assigned Claim handler: Mara Mustermann".
   - Believes: "I should be able to change this right here."
   - Does: looks next to her name for an action.
   - Feels: a bit rushed, wants it over with.
2. Opens the picker.
   - Sees: a dialog, "CLM-... is with Mara Mustermann", choice of A handler or A team queue, a search, a list.
   - Believes: "Everyone I can pick is here, and anyone missing has a reason."
   - Does: searches or scans, picks Lena, types a short reason.
   - Feels: in control if she can see who is away and why.
3. Confirms.
   - Sees: "CLM-... will move from Mara to Lena. Lena will be notified." Back / Yes, reassign.
   - Believes: "This is the last step, nothing has happened yet."
   - Does: presses Yes, reassign.
   - Feels: sure, because it says who hears about it.
4. Done.
   - Sees: toast, the handler field now says Lena, the activity log has the change and the reason.
   - Believes: "It is off my desk, Lena knows."
   - Does: moves on to her next claim.
   - Feels: relieved.

### B. Take a colleague's claim
1. Mara opens Nina's claim (same team) because the client is calling.
2. Sees "Take claim" under Nina's name. Presses it.
3. Confirm: "will move from Nina Feld to you and show in your claims. Nina Feld will be notified."
4. Toast "Claim taken". Handler is Mara. Claim is in My claims.

### C. Not allowed
1. Mara opens another team's claim, or one above her authority.
2. Instead of an action she sees why, and who can move it (AC-04, AC-05).
3. She contacts that manager outside the system (assumption: there is no "request" action).

### D. Choose the handler while registering (FNOL Summary)
1. Mara reaches the Summary step of a new claim.
2. Sees "Claim handler: Mara Mustermann (you)" with Change handler.
3. Picks Lena. The line now says Lena, "They will be notified when you submit", and Keep it myself.
4. Submits. The new claim is Lena's. Lena gets a notification.

### E. Before leave (claims list, bulk)
1. Mara filters the claims list to her claims, ticks them all.
2. Presses "Reassign N claims". Dialog lists the claims, says which are left out and why.
3. Picks Lena, confirms. Toast "N claims reassigned". The list refreshes. Lena gets one notification.

### F. Manager rebalances (dashboard)
1. Tobias opens Team workload, View claims of Nina, row menu, Reassign.
2. Picks Mara, confirms ("Mara and Nina will be notified").
3. Toast. Nina's count goes down, Mara's goes up. Both get a bell notification.

## Pain points

- Picking the wrong person: names alone do not say team, availability or authority (AC-02).
- Someone missing from the list with no reason. "Where is Sabine?" (DESIGN DECISION RA-PICK-1 in AC-02).
- Authority: a high-value claim given to someone who cannot settle it (AC-05, Confluence 3302433795 BR29 "financial transfers remain subject to BR29").
- Open tasks do not follow the claim. "A change of Claim owner does not automatically change an existing task" (Confluence 3302433795, post-creation reassignment). Before leave, the handler's open tasks stay with them.
- The losing handler is surprised. They did not act, so the notification is their only clue (AC-06).
- Silent failure: the save fails and the user walks away thinking it worked (AC-09, Confluence 3302433795 BR17.2 "the user is told why").
- Bulk: one closed or foreign claim should not block the batch, but the user must know what was left out (AC-11, RA-BULK-1).
- Registration: picking a handler on the last step and then going back to fix something (assumption: very common in FNOL).
- Routing may later win back. In the German CTR stream a claim's handler is only set by routing; a direct reassign would be overwritten (Confluence 3366776946, K-148 / K-149). For Commercial this is still open (assumption).
- Production today has no reassignment at all. The handler is a read-only field (`frontend/apps/claims-management-overview/src/app/pages/general-information/general-information.component.html:37-41`). So this is a new habit for users.

## Needs

- At the trigger: the action sits next to the handler name, where they already look.
- In the picker: who each person is (team), who is unavailable and why, who is above authority, a search, team queues as a clear alternative.
- Before commit: a plain confirm that names the claim, from whom, to whom, and who will be told.
- After commit: a toast, the handler field updated in place, an activity log entry with the reason.
- When not allowed: the reason, and the name of who can do it.
- When things fail: the dialog stays, the choice stays, the error is visible without scrolling.
- At registration: the pick survives going back and forth, and the final screen says who got the claim.
- In bulk: the count, the list, what is left out and why, the picker reachable without scrolling past every row.
- For the people on the receiving end: a bell notification that says who did it.

## Checks (owner's rules, applied to every step)

- Visible signal after every action: toast plus the handler field plus the activity log.
- Nothing reads as cancel or did-nothing: "Keep it myself", "Back" in the confirm, "Try again".
- Confirm before commit, toast after. Registration is the stated exception: Submit is the commit (RA-FNOL-1).
- Stay in context: dialog over the page, no navigation away.
- Same thing looks the same: one dialog for every entry point (claim, list, dashboard, FNOL).
- Plain language, names not IDs, no jargon.
- Only existing NDBX and app components: nx-radio, nx-formfield, nx-message, nx-spinner, nxTable, ConfirmDialogComponent, ToastService, EmptyStateComponent.

## Audit 2026-11-17

Walked on localhost:4262 with Playwright, as Mara (handler) and Tobias (manager), each AC's scenario applied through the app's own scenario service.
Screenshots: `/tmp/ux-audit/22666-<scenario>-<step>.png`.
Result: 20 scenarios, 13 pass, 7 fail.

### RA-OWN-01 Reassign own claim to a handler: pass
- Reassign sits under the handler name. Dialog, Sabine greyed "Out of office until 28-11-2026", confirm over the dialog, toast, field now Lena, activity log shows "Lena Zorn: Needs a cyber specialist" (RA-LOG-01 shot).
- Problem: confirm says "Lena Zorn will be notified", toast then says "Both handlers have been notified".
  - Why it hurts: two different promises in ten seconds (Check: same thing looks the same; plain language). Mara is the actor, she is not notified.
  - Fix: build the toast text from the same `told` list the confirm uses, `reassign-claim-modal.component.ts:199-205`, and drop the fixed sentence in `claim-assignment-actions.component.ts:101-104`.
- Problem: in the dialog, the status ("Out of office", "Above their handling authority") replaces the team in the same brackets.
  - Why it hurts: Mara loses who the person is exactly when she is deciding (Need: who each person is).
  - Fix: keep the team as the label and show the reason as a second line, like the blocked reason under the handler on Claim Overview (`claim-assignment-actions.component.ts:53-57`).

### RA-OWN-02 Reassign to a team queue: pass
- Two queues shown with their manager. Confirm says "Tobias Grau will be notified".
- Problem: toast says "now with Property claims team. Both handlers have been notified". A queue has no handler. The field then reads "Property claims team" without "queue", while the confirm said "the Property claims team queue".
  - Why it hurts: "Has anyone got it?" (Pain: losing handler surprised; Check: same wording everywhere).
  - Fix: one label helper for queue targets, the one already in `fnol-handler-picker.component.ts:33` (adds " queue" to team names), used by field, confirm and toast.

### RA-OWN-03 Back, Cancel, search with no match: pass
- Back from confirm keeps "Reassigning to Lena Zorn". Cancel closes, nothing changes. "No one matches "zzz"." shows the shared empty state.

### RA-TAKE-01 Take a colleague's claim: pass
- Confirm "will move from Nina Feld to you and show in your claims. Nina Feld will be notified." Toast "Claim taken". Claim shows in My claims.
- Problem: no way to tell Nina why. Reassign has a reason field, Take does not.
  - Why it hurts: Nina only gets "was moved to Mara" (Pain: losing handler surprised).
  - Fix: open the same reassign dialog pre-picked with "you" and its Reason field (`reassign-claim-modal.component.html:120-127`), or add an optional textarea to the confirm.

### RA-BLK-01 Another team's claim: pass
- "Held by the Marine and liability team. Ask Marcus Fehr (team manager) to move it to you." with an info icon, under the name.

### RA-BLK-02 Above authority, manager can: pass
- Mara: "Above your handling authority (up to EUR 500,000). Ask Tobias Grau (team manager) to assign it." Tobias: Reassign.

### RA-BLK-03 Own closed claim: fail
- What the user sees: CLM-2024-027, "Assigned Claim handler Mara Mustermann" and nothing else. No action, no reason.
- Why it hurts: the action is there on every other claim and silently gone here (Check: visible signal; Need: when not allowed, say why). The service already has the sentence "Closed claims cannot be reassigned. Reopen it first." (`mock-assignment.service.ts:63`), but the template hides it (`claim-assignment-actions.component.ts:53`, `claim().status !== 'Closed'`).
- Fix: show `reassignCheck().reason` in the same info-icon line used for AC-04 (`claim-assignment-actions.component.ts:53-57`).

### RA-MGR-01 Manager, high-value claim: pass
- Handlers greyed "Above their handling authority".
- Problem: the text reads "Lena Zorn (Above their handling authority (up to 500,000))". Double brackets, no currency, while Mara's own line says "EUR 500,000".
  - Fix: same currency format as `mock-assignment.service.ts:99`, and the second-line layout from RA-OWN-01.
- Problem: Tobias is listed as a target for a claim he is moving himself ("Tobias Grau (Property claims team manager)"). Fine if he means "give it to me", but the list also offers the current queue.
  - Fix: label him "Tobias Grau (you)", like the FNOL picker does (`fnol-handler-picker.component.ts:31`).

### RA-NOTIF-01 Manager reassigns from dashboard: pass
- Row menu Reassign opens the same dialog. Confirm "Mara Mustermann and Nina Feld will be notified." Counts: Mara 7 to 8, Nina 4 to 3. Mara's bell: "CLM-2024-022 was assigned to you by Tobias Grau" under Needs you; the claim is on her dashboard.
- Not checkable: Nina is not a persona in the prototype, so her "was moved" notice cannot be seen.
- Problem: toast again says "Both handlers have been notified", while Tobias is neither. Same fix as RA-OWN-01.

### RA-ERR-01 Loading: pass
- "Loading who can take the claim…" with a spinner, Reassign disabled, Cancel closes.

### RA-ERR-02 List fails: pass
- "We couldn't load the list of handlers." with Try again, which shows the spinner again.
- Problem: the search box and the Reason field stay active with nothing to pick.
  - Why it hurts: invites typing that leads nowhere (Check: no stumbles).
  - Fix: hide search and reason while `listState() !== 'ready'`, like the bulk closure dialog shows only the message in its error step (`bulk-closure-modal.component.html:22-26`).

### RA-ERR-03 Save fails: fail
- What the user sees: after Yes, reassign, the dialog stays with the pick and reason kept, but the error message sits at the bottom of the scrolling body and is cut off ("The reassignment didn't go through, so the claim is ..."). At 1440 x 900 it is not fully visible.
- Why it hurts: the only signal that nothing happened is out of view; it looks like the button did nothing (Pain: silent failure; Check: visible signal).
- Fix: put the save error at the top of the body, above the radio choice, and scroll it into view, like the error-first order in `bulk-closure-modal.component.html:22-26`.

### RA-EMPTY-01 Nobody can take it: pass
- "No one else can take this claim right now. Ask your team manager." in both tabs, shared empty state.

### RA-BULK-01 Bulk before leave: fail
- What the user sees: 10 ticked, button "Reassign 10 claims". Dialog title "Reassign 9 claims", then a 9-row table of claim, client, and "Current handler: Mara Mustermann" on every row. The note "1 of the selected claims is not yours to move" and the handler picker are below the fold.
- Why it hurts:
  - 10 becomes 9 with no name; the skipped one is CLM-2024-027, which IS hers, it is closed. "Not yours to move" is wrong (Need: what is left out and why).
  - The picker, the actual task, is hidden under a list she already knows (Need: picker reachable without scrolling).
  - A column with the same name nine times adds nothing.
- Fix: reuse the bulk closure pattern: one nx-message summary on top ("9 of 10 can be reassigned. 1 is closed."), then the per-item list with a badge per claim (`bulk-closure-modal.component.html:27-60`). Put the picker above the list, or collapse the list.
- After confirm: toast "9 claims reassigned. Now with Lena Zorn." and the My claims list refreshes to only the closed claim. Pass.

### RA-BULK-02 Bulk with others' claims: fail
- Skipped note shows ("2 of the selected claims are not yours to move").
- What the user sees: "Mara Mustermann (Property claims team)" is in the list of people she can give her own claims to.
- Why it hurts: an option that does nothing, and the confirm then says "Lena Zorn and the current handlers will be notified" when the current handler is herself (Check: no stumbles).
- Fix: exclude the actor in bulk the way single mode does via `currentOwner` (`reassign-claim-modal.component.ts:142`), and name people in the confirm.

### RA-BULK-03 Nothing is mine: pass
- Info toast "Nothing to reassign. None of the selected claims are yours to move. Their handler or team manager can move them."

### RA-FNOL-01 Registration pick and undo: pass
- "Claim handler: Mara Mustermann (you)" with Change handler. Dialog "Choose who handles this claim" with no confirm (RA-FNOL-1). Line then shows "Lena Zorn. They will be notified when you submit." with Keep it myself. Keep it myself goes back to "(you)".

### RA-FNOL-02 Pick, go Back, return: fail
- What the user sees: picks the Property claims team queue, presses Back to Reserves, returns to Summary. The line says "Mara Mustermann (you)" again. No message.
- Why it hurts: the decision is lost silently; she may submit a claim to herself that she meant to route (Need: the pick survives going back and forth). Cause: `fnol-handler-picker.component.ts:55-57` resets on destroy.
- Fix: keep the choice in `FnolHandlerChoiceService` for the whole wizard and clear it only on Submit or on wizard Cancel, the same lifetime as the other FnolState fields.

### RA-FNOL-03 Submit to the chosen handler: fail
- The new claim's overview says "Assigned Claim handler: Lena Zorn". Good.
- What the user sees on the post-submit Summary: "1 claim opened: CL-..." and, under General configurations, "Claim handler: Mara Mustermann (you)" with a live Change handler button.
- Why it hurts: the last screen says the opposite of what happened and offers to change something that is already saved (Check: visible signal; no stumbles). `announce()` clears the choice before the screen re-renders (`fnol-handler-choice.service.ts:41`).
- Also: the Reason typed at registration is stored (`fnol-handler-choice.service.ts:27`) but never used. Not in the notification, not in the activity log.
- Fix: in the submitted state, show the handler as read-only text in the success message ("Assigned to Lena Zorn, who has been notified"), following the success nx-message already at `step-summary.component.html:14-19`; hide the picker when `submitted`; pass the reason into the activity log like `mock-assignment.service.ts:185`.

### RA-KEY-01 Keyboard and screen reader: fail (minor)
- What the user sees: Reassign and Take claim are `<a tabindex="0">` with no href and no role (checked in the DOM). Enter works through a handler; Space does not; a screen reader announces plain text, not a button.
- Why it hurts: CLAUDE.md accessibility rules, and it is the only entry point on the claim.
- Fix: use a real button with link styling, the `nxPlainButton` already used for "Select all matching" in the claims list bulk bar (`claims-list.component.html:80`).

### Across all scenarios
- Open tasks: the overview shows "No tasks found" for these claims, so the prototype never shows what happens to the handler's open tasks. Confluence 3302433795 says they do not move with the claim. Before-leave bulk reassign should say so in the confirm (assumption, needs product decision).
- Routing may override a manual move (Confluence 3366776946, K-149). Needs a decision for Commercial before build.
- AC-12 (Figma, German text, small screens) is not built, as the ticket says.
